NaVault

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

NaVault does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data, with anyone, ever. It has no INTERNET permission — it is not technically capable of sending data anywhere, not to us, not to any third party, not to an analytics or crash-reporting service. Everything you store in NaVault stays on your device, encrypted, until you choose to export, back up, or share it yourself.

What NaVault stores, and where

All vault content — passwords, bank account details, card details, documents, notes, and passes — is stored only in a local encrypted database on your device (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your master password via Argon2id). This data:

  • Never leaves your device automatically.
  • Is never sent to any server — NaVault has none; there is no backend of any kind.
  • Is never shared with any third-party service, SDK, or analytics/advertising network.
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service by NaVault itself (Android's own system backup is explicitly disabled for this app).

Local backups

If you enable backups (Settings > Backup Folder), NaVault writes an encrypted copy of your vault database to a folder you choose on your device's own storage, on a rolling 7-day retention schedule. This file never leaves your device unless you move it yourself — NaVault takes no part in that.

Permissions NaVault requests, and why

Permission Why it's needed
Camera To scan documents/ID cards directly in-app. Captured images are processed and stored locally only.
Biometric / Fingerprint To unlock the vault with your device's fingerprint/face unlock instead of typing your master password every time. Biometric data is handled entirely by the Android OS (Keystore) — NaVault never sees or stores it.
Storage / manage external storage To let you choose a backup folder, restore from a backup file, and export or import CSV files you explicitly select. NaVault only reads/writes files you point it to.

NaVault does not request the Internet permission, location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or any other permission not listed above.

On-device features that might sound like they need a network — they don't

  • Text recognition (OCR) on scanned documents uses a model bundled inside the app itself, not a cloud API. No image or text is ever uploaded anywhere.
  • Optional AI-assisted text import runs a small language model entirely on-device. No text you paste is sent anywhere.
  • Barcode/QR generation and scanning for Passes is done entirely locally.

Data sharing

The only way data leaves NaVault is if you explicitly choose to:

  • Share a document/page to another app you pick (e.g. WhatsApp, Gmail) via Android's own share sheet.
  • Download a decrypted copy of a document to your device's Downloads folder.
  • Export your data as a plaintext JSON/CSV file, which you then control entirely.

None of these actions are automatic — each requires you to explicitly tap share/download/export.

Children's privacy

NaVault is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age — it does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Since NaVault has no way to contact you directly (no accounts, no email collection), please check this page periodically if you want to stay informed.
